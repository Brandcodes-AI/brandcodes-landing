## Design Context

### Users
Primary users are B2B decision-makers and operators in brand owners, manufacturers, retailers, and GS1/channel partners who need to deploy GS1 Digital Link and 2D barcode workflows at scale.

Secondary users are end consumers scanning product codes for information, support, and authenticity checks.

Core jobs to be done:
- Migrate to GS1 Sunrise 2027 with minimal operational risk.
- Generate and manage product-specific URLs and GS1-compliant QR/2D barcodes across large SKU catalogs.
- Connect packaging scans to support, multilingual content, and measurable engagement outcomes.

### Brand Personality
Trustworthy, technical, pragmatic.

The interface should signal enterprise readiness, compliance confidence, and implementation speed. Tone should be clear and direct, with minimal hype.

### Aesthetic Direction
Modern B2B infrastructure aesthetic: structured layouts, high signal-to-noise, and clear hierarchy that prioritizes credibility and action over visual novelty.

Visual language can reference scanning/data patterns, but decorative motifs should never compete with comprehension or conversion.

### Design Principles
- Lead with clarity: explain the offer in one pass without forcing users to decode jargon.
- Prioritize trust signals: compliance, proof, and operational reliability must be visually and editorially prominent.
- Reduce decision friction: limit choices at each step and emphasize one primary action.
- Use progressive disclosure: reveal technical depth in layers rather than presenting all complexity at once.
- Keep visuals purposeful: every pattern, badge, and animation must reinforce meaning, not add noise.
